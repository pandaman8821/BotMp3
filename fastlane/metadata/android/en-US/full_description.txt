BotMp3 is a local music player for Android with automatic library scanning,
unlimited playlists, a cover-art editor, play-count charts, online trends and an
AI DJ you can command in writing.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Works 100 % offline: your music and your data never leave the device.
• No ads, no trackers, no accounts.
• AI keys are stored encrypted in the Android Keystore.
• Text is only sent to the AI when you ask for it, and it is fully optional.

AUTOMATIC LIBRARY
• On launch it finds the music on your device (MediaStore) and adds new tracks with
  title, artist, album, year, genre and duration.
• Album art is extracted and applied automatically.
• Plays MP3, M4A, M4B, AAC, OGG, OGA, OPUS, FLAC, WAV, AIF and AIFF.
• Global search by text, genre, artist, year and tags, with filters and favourites.
• Unlimited playlists with JSON export/import for backups.
• "Free up space": clears the cache and replaces copies with the original files.

SIMPLE EDITING
• Full metadata: title, artist, album, year, genre and tags.
• AI auto-fill: fixes typos and completes missing data.
• Original album art with a single tap.
• Cover editor: brightness, contrast, saturation, rotation, crop, filters, text or
  logo, and export.
• Full-screen cover art during playback.

MUSIC AI THAT RUNS COMMANDS (optional, bring your own key)
Works with OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude.
• Runs what you type: "play cumbia", "play the Fiesta playlist",
  "create a 20-song workout playlist", "add this song to Fiesta",
  "change the genre to Rock", "set volume to 80 %", "speed 1.5x"…
• Explained recommendations based on your library and your mood.
• Automatic playlists, natural-language search and AI DJ mode.
• Song and lyric idea generation.
• Many commands work offline and without a key thanks to the local interpreter.
• Always up-to-date models: pick a recommended one or type any new model id.

PERSONAL CHARTS
• Built-in play history with per-period rankings (all time, last month, last week).
• Four charts: songs, artists, albums and genres, with podium medals and a button
  to play the top 10.

ONLINE TRENDS
• The most played songs right now in ten countries, from a public source that
  needs no key and no account.
• If a trending song is already in your library it is flagged and plays on tap;
  otherwise it opens a web search.
• Configurable daily notification (you choose the hour) with the top 3, plus an
  instant notification button and a copy-list action.

PLAYBACK
• Notification with cover art and controls: pause, next and previous also on the
  lock screen, headset buttons, watch and Android Auto.
• Shuffle, repeat, 0.5x–2x speed, volume and keep-screen-on.
• Resumes where you left off and plays nicely with system audio.

LOOK
• "Liquid Glass" effect on bars and panels, with real backdrop blur that picks up
  the colours of the album art currently playing.
• Adjustable glass intensity, automatically disabled in battery-saver mode.
• Modern dark theme and light theme.

SETTINGS & DIAGNOSTICS
• Permissions, notifications and background behaviour, plus a diagnostics screen
  that checks why lock-screen controls may not respond.

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 7.0 or newer (arm64-v8a).
• Tuned for POCO X7 Pro (1220×2712, 120 Hz): light animations, low battery usage.

OPEN SOURCE
• GPL-3.0-or-later. Source code, issues and build instructions are in the project
  repository.

ANTI-FEATURES
• NonFreeNet: the AI features are optional and connect to non-free services
  (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude) using the user's own key.
  The trends list uses a free public service with no key. The app is fully
  functional without enabling any of these connections.
